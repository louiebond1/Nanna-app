# Colour Clash: house rules (Uno-style, two players)

This is an original version of the familiar colour-matching shedding game. The name, card faces and wording are our own.

- **Deck:** 108 cards.
  - Four colours, each with its own shape so you never need to tell colours apart: Red ▲, Yellow ●, Green ■, Blue ◆.
  - Each colour has one 0, two each of 1–9, two Skips, two Reverses and two Draw Twos.
  - Plus four Wilds and four Wild Draw Fours.
- **Start:**
  - 7 cards each. The first player is chosen at random by the server.
  - The first face-up card is always a number card, so no effect applies at the start. This is a simplification.
- **Your turn:** play one card that matches the colour to match, or the top card's number or symbol. A Wild matches anything.
- **Drawing:**
  - If you can't play, or choose not to, draw one card.
  - If that card can be played, you may play it at once or keep it and end your turn. Otherwise your turn ends.
  - If the deck runs out, the discard pile (except its top card) is reshuffled. If there's truly nothing left to draw and nothing to play, you pass.
- **Action cards with two players:**
  - **Skip** and **Reverse** both mean the other player misses a turn, so you go again.
  - **Draw Two:** the other player picks up 2 and misses a turn, so you go again.
  - **Wild:** you choose the colour.
  - **Wild Draw Four:** choose the colour. The other player picks up 4 and misses a turn.
- **Wild Draw Four legality:** it can be played at any time. There's no "challenge" rule. This is a deliberate simplification.
- **No stacking:** you can't answer a Draw Two or Draw Four with another one. The pick-up happens straight away.
- **Last card:** the game announces it automatically. Nobody has to call it in time, and there's no speed penalty.
- **Scoring:**
  - The first player out wins the round and scores the cards left in the other hand: numbers count their face value, Skip, Reverse and Draw Two count 20, and Wilds count 50.
  - If you go out on a Draw Two or Draw Four, the pick-up happens first and those cards count.
- **Length:** one round (the default), first to 200 points, or first to 500 points. Both players agree in the lobby. A new round alternates who starts.
- **Privacy:**
  - The deck order and the other player's hand are never sent to your device.
  - The card you draw, and the cards you're made to pick up, are named only to you.
