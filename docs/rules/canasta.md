# Canasta: house rules (two players)

This is standard two-player Canasta (as described on pagat.com), with the simplifications listed at the end. Canasta is a traditional public-domain game.

- **Cards:** two 52-card decks plus four Jokers, 108 in all. Twos and Jokers are **wild**.
- **Deal:**
  - 15 cards each, chosen at random by the server. The first player alternates each hand.
  - The first face-up discard is the first natural card that isn't a Three. Anything skipped goes to the bottom of the stock.
- **Red Threes:** when dealt or drawn, they're put down automatically and replaced. They're worth 100 each (800 for all four). That counts for you if you've melded this hand, and against you if you haven't.
- **Your turn:** **draw two cards** from the stock (two-player rule), or take the discard pile (below). Then meld if you like, and discard one card.
- **Melds:**
  - Three or more cards of one rank (Fours to Aces).
  - At least two natural cards, and at most three wild cards.
  - One meld per rank: further cards are added to it.
  - Black Threes may only be melded when going out, and never with wild cards.
- **First meld minimum:** your first meld of each hand must be worth at least a minimum, based on your total so far:
  - below 0: 15 points;
  - 0–1495: 50;
  - 1500–2995: 90;
  - 3000 or more: 120.
  Several melds laid in the same turn count together.
- **Card values:** Joker 50; Ace and Two 20; King down to Eight 10; Seven down to Four 5; black Three 5.
- **Taking the discard pile:**
  - The top card must go straight into a meld: either with two natural cards of its rank from your hand, or (when the pile isn't frozen) onto your existing meld of that rank, or with one natural and one wild.
  - Then the rest of the pile joins your hand.
  - The pile is **frozen** to you until you've made your first meld, and for both players once a wild card has been discarded onto it. While frozen, it can only be taken with two natural cards matching the top card.
  - It can never be taken when a wild card or a black Three is on top.
- **Canastas:** a meld of seven or more cards. A natural canasta (no wild cards) is worth 500; a mixed one 300.
- **Going out:**
  - You need **two canastas** (two-player rule).
  - You go out by melding all your cards, or by melding all but one and discarding it. You're asked to confirm.
  - Without two canastas you can't reduce your hand below two cards, so you're never left unable to discard.
- **Scoring a hand:** the points of your melded cards, plus canasta bonuses, plus or minus red Threes, plus 100 for going out, minus the points of the cards left in your hand. The full sum is shown for both players.
- **Length:** first to 5000 (standard), first to 2500, or one hand. Both players agree in the lobby.
- **Help on screen:**
  - Suggested moves, for example "Meld your 3 Kings" or "Meld your Sixes and Kings together (50 points)".
  - The points value of the cards you've selected, and your first-meld minimum.
  - A plain-English reason whenever a move isn't allowed.
- **Simplifications** (on purpose):
  - There's no concealed going-out bonus.
  - You don't ask permission to go out (that's for partnership games).
  - When the stock can't supply two cards, the hand simply ends with no going-out bonus.
  - The skipped starting cards go under the stock rather than freezing the pile.
